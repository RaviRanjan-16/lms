📚 About Our Library

Welcome to our **Library Management System**, designed to efficiently manage the **physical books** available in our **college/school library**. Our goal is to ensure a smooth experience for students and faculty by making book **borrowing, returning, and tracking** more organized and accessible.

📖 Our Library Features

  • ✔ **Borrow & Return Books Easily**
  • ✔ **Track Issued Books & Due Dates**
  • ✔ **Manage Book Categories & Authors**
  • ✔ **Efficient Student & Faculty Management**
